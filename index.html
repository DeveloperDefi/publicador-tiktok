Real estate data, published on schedule.

Nathanael Estivalett Publicador is the publishing tool behind @nathanaelestivalett. Every day it takes the real estate carousels and short videos Nathanael has already produced and approved, and posts them to his own TikTok account at fixed times — so the same content that goes to Instagram reaches TikTok too, without anyone re-uploading it by hand.

Single-user tool: it is operated by its owner, for his own account only. There is no sign-up and no third-party accounts.

How it works

  1. 1. Content is produced and approved

    Each day Nathanael publishes one image carousel about the Brazilian real estate market (every figure carries its source on the card) and, some days, a short captioned video. Nothing reaches the tool before he approves it.

  2. 2. The tool prepares it for TikTok

    Carousel images are converted to JPEG in the 9:16 frame TikTok displays. The Instagram caption is shortened to what fits on a TikTok screen: the opening line, the call to action and the hashtags. Source credits are never removed.

  3. 3. It is sent to his TikTok account

    Using the TikTok Content Posting API, photos are sent by URL from a verified domain and videos are uploaded as files. Today every post arrives in his TikTok inbox as a draft, which he reviews and posts from the TikTok app.

  4. 4. Every post is recorded

    The tool keeps a log of what was sent and when, so the same item is never posted twice and nothing is silently skipped.

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, with owner-only file permissions. The tool does not read followers, comments, messages or any other user's data. See the Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Can I use this tool for my own account?

No. It is private software that serves a single account, @nathanaelestivalett. There is no sign-up and no login for the public.

Does it create content?

No. It only publishes content Nathanael has already produced and approved for Instagram.

Does it post on behalf of anyone else?

Never. It only posts to the owner's own TikTok account.

How do I stop it?

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.
