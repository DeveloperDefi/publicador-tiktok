Nathanael Estivalett Publicador

A single-user tool that publishes its owner's own content to his own TikTok account.

This application is operated by and for one person: Nathanael Estivalett (@nathanaelestivalett). It takes content he has already produced — image carousels with data about the Brazilian real estate market, and short captioned videos — and posts it to his own TikTok profile on a fixed daily schedule.

It does not sign up other users, does not read or store data belonging to anyone else, and never posts on another person's behalf.
